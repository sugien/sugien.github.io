Privacy policy

Study Library is a personal tool used only by its owner, with rclone, to read and write files in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • Google user data accessed: the files in the owner's own Drive, through the Google Drive API.
  • Use: copying the owner's study files between their Drive and their own devices. Nothing else.
  • Storage: the access token stays in an encrypted rclone configuration on the owner's own devices.
  • Sharing: no data is collected, sold, shared with or shown to anyone else. There is no server, analytics or advertising.
  • Revoking: the owner can remove access at any time in their Google Account under third-party connections.
